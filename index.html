These skills matter more, not less.

A customer says yes when someone gets their problem, earns their trust, talks about the result they want, and gets our teams lined up to deliver it.

What this asks of people

Understand.

When the doing gets cheaper, it helps to know where the value sits for the customer. Energy cost. Uptime. Throughput.

Trust.

Customers can pull the analysis themselves. What still helps is being clear about the trade-offs, and staying close to the people who decide.

A second look.

Answers are easy to get now. Someone still needs to see if they hold up, and keep up as the tools change.
