What changed in the AI era

Understand.

When execution gets cheaper, the advantage moves to where value sits in the customer’s process. Energy cost. Uptime. Throughput.

Trust.

Customers can generate the analysis themselves. Credibility decides: explain the trade-offs, and build the relationship with the people who decide.

Judge.

Output is easy to produce. Someone still has to decide what is right, challenge it, and adapt as the tools change.
